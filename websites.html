Questions

What's the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter is a single page on a free web address (yourname.netlify.app), with free hosting. Business has up to 5 pages and your own domain, like yourbusiness.co.za, with the domain and hosting included for the first year.

What happens after the first year?

Business sites have a yearly renewal for the domain and hosting, usually about R100–R200. Starter sites have no yearly fee.

I don't know what I need. Can you help?
